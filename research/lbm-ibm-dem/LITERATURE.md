# Papers, people and codes: how to use the evidence

The archived literature review has 47 numbered sources (papers, code and institutional pages); the strategy has 26, with overlap. The solver's IBM methods note is a third bibliography. Sources listed below are entry points, not independent replications of this prototype. Publisher papers are linked rather than redistributed. Dates and capability statements in the original reports are historical.

| Evidence | Primary sources | What it can establish |
|---|---|---|
| Microplastic hydrodynamics | [Zhang et al., cylindrical settling](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.marpolbul.2023.115438); [Ji et al., films](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.marpolbul.2024.116493); [Shen et al., settling and rising](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envpol.2024.125107); [Yuan et al., fibres](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eti.2024.103951); [He et al., channels](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecoenv.2024.117308) | Shape, orientation, density and drag. Isolated settling does not validate frictional contacts, fibre entanglement or clogging. |
| Interfacial and macroscale MP applications | [Lehmann et al., raindrops](https://doi.org/10.1186/s43591-021-00018-8); [Lehmann et al., bubbles](https://doi.org/10.1186/s43591-023-00053-7); [Bai et al., wetland transport](https://doi.org/10.3390/su18168240); [Liu and Gekle, EGU abstract](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-egu26-13225) | Distinguish point-particle or continuum models from contact-resolved DEM. The last item is a conference abstract. |
| Clogging, retention and interfaces | [Xu et al., microplastic flow-cell experiments](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0198462); [Zhou et al., deposition](https://doi.org/10.3390/pr9030465); [Wang et al., fractures](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrmms.2025.106259); [Wang et al., constrictions](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025WR042425); [Naga et al., wetting](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2026.11865) | Experimental targets and transferable physics. Mineral-particle parameter sets are not polymer calibration. |
| Coupled particle mechanics | [Ladd](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022112094001771); [Noble and Torczynski](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129183198001084); [Feng et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/nme.2114); [Galindo-Torres](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2013.06.004); [Rettinger and Rüde](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2022.110942); [Vlogman et al., resolved/unresolved comparison](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0253313) | Distinguish IBM from partially saturated-cell/IMB coupling, and general LBM–DEM from microplastic-specific validation. |
| IBM and its inertia correction | [Uhlmann](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2005.03.017); [Wu and Shu](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2008.11.019); [Suzuki and Inamuro](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compfluid.2011.05.011); [Breugem](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2012.02.026); [Kempe and Fröhlich](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2012.01.021); [Wang, Feng and Qu, explicit DEM–IBM–LBM prior art](https://doi.org/10.1002/nag.3035); [Lācis et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2015.10.041); [Bao et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2017.06.041); [Gu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0351280) | No-slip residual, interior momentum, and work-consistent transfer are different checks. Generic implicit/matrix-free IBM is not new. |
| Wet contact and narrow gaps | [Nguyen and Ladd](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.66.046708); [Costa et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.92.053012); [Fai and Rycroft](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.08865); [Zhu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmultiphaseflow.2023.104411) | Check unresolved resistance, relative motion and physical collision duration. Do not transplant 3D sphere coefficients to 2D cylinders. |
| Acceleration and alternatives | [Adaptive light particles](https://doi.org/10.1002/fld.5034); [octree IBM](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2022.111669); [CPU/GPU adaptive IBM](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2025.109674); [multiresolution LBM](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2022.111670); [contact error control](https://doi.org/10.1515/cmam-2015-0014); [rigid multiblob preconditioning](https://doi.org/10.2140/camcos.2016.11.217); [pore-flow preconditioning](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2024.113541); [LEDDS](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2026.110294) | Prior art, candidate baselines and possible design routes; no head-to-head timing is implied. |

**Researchers to follow by question.** Microplastics and transport: Stephan Gekle, Christina Bogner (experimental soil work), Jinfeng Zhang, Chaoqun Ji, Xiaoteng Shen, Fangyang Yuan and Moritz Lehmann. Coupled granular methods: Yuntian Feng, Min Wang, Christoph Rettinger, Ulrich Rüde, Sergio Galindo-Torres, Mathias Krause, Peng Hou and Siyuan Wang. IBM and wet-contact numerics: Markus Uhlmann, Wim-Paul Breugem, Tobias Kempe, Jochen Fröhlich, Pedro Costa, Thomas Fai and Chris Rycroft. These are technical connections, not collaborators or endorsements.

**Software shortlist.** [waLBerla/mesa-pd](https://github.com/lssfau/walberla) for distributed resolved coupling; [OpenLB](https://www.openlb.net/) for particle-framework comparisons; [LEDDS](https://gitlab.com/unigehpfs/ledds) for GPU baselines on compatible physics; [Wang's IBM/IMB code](https://github.com/sacewangmin/3D-DEM-LBM-IMB) for a closer coupling-method comparison; [MechSys](https://github.com/Axtal/mechsys) for general shapes; [LBDEMcoupling](https://github.com/ParticulateFlow/LBDEMcoupling-public), [Wang's extension](https://github.com/YangHuaWSY/LBM-PSM-DEM-Extension) and [LAMMPS-LBM-PSM](https://github.com/timnajuch/LAMMPS-LBM-PSM) for alternative granular stacks. [FluidX3D](https://github.com/ProjectPhysX/FluidX3D), [ESPResSo](https://github.com/espressomd/espresso) and [LBsoft](https://github.com/copmat/LBsoft) address adjacent problems; don't treat an adjacent solver as a drop-in frictional DEM benchmark. Check the specific version and licence before redistributing or benchmarking any external code.
