Baseline full source: 935f05c9f394c65742a6b829bebb4385e801c117. Remote contact branch: 3bbb9eeb8aa272f51bb125463cacf35fec8d3e23.
Scope: actual 2D equilibrium capillary patch + porous-root uptake + two-compartment storage + native spring-supported grain. The separate radial Richards model is a continuum verification reference, not LBM. No new species or force calibration: freeze stored profiles. No angular grains, flexible roots or whole-tree claims.
Review passes: coupling/conservation; hydraulic interpretation; evidence/reproducibility. Same assistant, no independent subagents.
Verification gates: regressions pass; water ledger <1e-16 m2; force residual <1.1e-7 N/m; contour/energy force discrepancy <2.5% (existing gate); curvature/Young-Laplace target <5% away from wall stencils; matched removal <1e-16 m2; zero uptake gives identical controls. Hydraulic timestep halving target <1% in outputs. Spatial checks keep the numerical wall angle fixed; separate recalibration from convergence. A physical effect must exceed numerical spread to be considered resolved, not merely have a consistent sign.
Additional test: closed hydraulic redistribution at fixed gap with matched total remaining water. Does the path difference vanish when pressure equilibrates? Solve common-pressure states without changing hydraulic coefficients.
Experiments: frozen ceramic fit predicts large-cup volume, incorporating small-cup parameter scatter (5 replicates; published SD). Saturated target <10% discrepancy is an engineering gate. Fixed-time vs fixed-volume endpoints are correlated, not independent replicates. Frozen saturated coefficient in unsaturated cases is explicitly an out-of-domain stress test: soil pressure and wet area unmeasured. Chronological tree holdouts use frozen species coefficients, target <20% discrepancy in means. Within-group SD is not uncertainty on the mean. External spruce data must not be passed off as matched-specimen validation.
Attempt independent bridge-force data; if inaccessible or 3D finite length geometry cannot be matched, record NOT RUN. No whole-model physical validation unless matched observations are available and executed.
