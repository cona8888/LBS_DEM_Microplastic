# Primary evidence and applicability

1. Kohne, J.M. (2005), Mini Suction Cups and Water-Extraction Effects on Preferential Solute Transport. Vadose Zone Journal, 4, 866–880. DOI: 10.2136/vzj2004.0111. Table 3. Six published means and SDs, five cups per size. Transcribed numerical observations, not replicate-level raw records. Cups in loam are a porous-root analogue for hydraulic access only, not living tissue. The small saturated cup was fitted previously. Only the large saturated cup is the primary frozen-parameter geometry holdout. The fixed-volume time endpoint is not counted as independent evidence. Unsaturated soil pressure at the cup is unreported in these observations.
2. Leuschner et al. (2003), Chemical composition of the periderm in relation to in situ water absorption rates of oak, beech and spruce fine roots. DOI: 10.1051/forest:2003071, Table II. Uses the existing reviewed transcription under calibration/data and stored species coefficients. 9 September was reserved previously. A chronological holdout at the same site is not an independent site or species validation. Reconstructed flux from Lp times pressure is not an independently measured flux pair. Publisher PDF was inaccessible in this session; this comparison relies on the project's earlier transcribed dataset, not a fresh transcription.
3. Rudinger et al. (1994), Hydraulic and osmotic properties of spruce roots. Journal of Experimental Botany, 45, 1413–1425. DOI: 10.1093/jxb/45.10.1413. Author/institutional abstract confirmed. Excised 2-year-old seedlings; steady and transient hydrostatic protocols have different conductivities and differ from osmotic tests. A cross-study spruce constant is NOT admitted as a matched validation observation. No numerical pass/fail was assigned to these mismatched specimens.
4. Liao et al. (2026), Wetting morphology and capillary force of liquid bridges between parallel cylinders: An experimental and numerical study. AIChE Journal 72, e70229. DOI: 10.1002/aic.70229. Publisher names Supporting Information_data.xlsx and Figures 5,7,8,9–13. Workbook values were not retrieved. Finite-volume, finite-length 3D bridges also need matched wet span/end effects. Independent bridge-force comparison NOT RUN. Surface Evolver values must never be classified as experiments.

No laboratory data were invented or regenerated from a fitted model. New simulations are explicitly numerical. Source tables and PDF prose are not reproduced wholesale. Research retrieval attempts/statuses are stored in results/retrieval.json.
