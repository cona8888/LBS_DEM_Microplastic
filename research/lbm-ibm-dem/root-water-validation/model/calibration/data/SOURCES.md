# Experimental observations and provenance

These are manual transcriptions of numerical observations reported in primary papers. They are not the authors' individual-root records or complete force traces. Original articles, supplementary files and images are not redistributed. All generated fields, curves and held-out predictions reside under `results/`, not `data/`.

**Glass data interpretation.** The site labels and 31° target follow the detailed results paragraph on the initial imperfection. Elsewhere in the same paper the left-side assignment is reversed and the three similar values are rounded to 30°. Accordingly the reproducible constraint is three sites near 30–31° and one near 39°, not the exceptional site’s exact position. The site split is an internal check, not an independent validation.

## Reserved mechanical observations

* Hueckel et al. measured vertical adhesion with a force balance on a three-cylinder arrangement (diameter 3.96 mm, initially wetted length 8.57 mm, 10 µL). Its ≈1.3 mN is not the force on a single 0.35 mm radius grain. Even correct length normalization does not repair the body count and geometry mismatch.
* Liao et al. (2026), [parallel-cylinder wetting and force measurements](https://doi.org/10.1002/aic.70229). The authors identify `Supporting Information_data.xlsx`; that workbook was not retrieved or used here. Reproduce their geometry, volume, moving axial footprint and end curvature before fitting forces. Do not use simulated curves as measured data.
* Kamchoom, Leung & Ng (2014), [centrifuge pull-out of transpiring root analogues](https://doi.org/10.1680/geolett.14.00086). Reserve for a deformable, many-grain soil/root contact model. Axial pull-out resistance is not the normal force opening an isolated liquid bridge.
