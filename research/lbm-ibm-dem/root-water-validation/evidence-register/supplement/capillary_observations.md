# Independent capillary bridge observations

The CSV adds six graph-read experimental force landmarks and four tabulated experimental cross-sectional areas. None of the observations were used to fit the current solver. The sphere observations can test a future 3D calculation. The zero-voltage cylinder observation supplies a measured initial area for a gravity-aware cross-sectional shape benchmark.

## Sphere force

Liévano, Velankar and McCarthy (2017) stretched an ethylene-glycol bridge between two acrylic spheres at 10 µm/s. Each radius was 1 mm. Figure 5A plots three runs at each of two dispensed volumes. The authors assume near-zero wetting angle. Table 1 gives surface tension 0.048 N/m and viscosity 0.015 Pa s at 20 °C. [Primary paper, DOI 10.1016/j.powtec.2017.02.053](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.powtec.2017.02.053) · [Author-hosted PDF, p. 22](https://sites.pitt.edu/~velankar/www/papers/ptLievano2017.pdf#page=5)

At D/R = 0.2, 0.4 and 0.6 we read the approximate centre of each coloured experimental band on the two linear axes. Original units are F/(σR), D/R and V/R³. Here one unit of force is 48 µN and one unit of volume is 1 µL. The CSV converts attractive magnitudes to total force and rounds to the nearest µN. The ±0.15 F/(σR) allowance (±7.2 µN) describes conservative graphical reading precision. It is separate from instrumental uncertainty and replicate scatter, which these readings cannot estimate separately. The last plotted point in a run is the last measured separation before rupture. Table 2 contains computed rupture-distance estimates and was excluded. The current 2D force in N/m cannot be scored against this total force in N. [Primary Figure 5A, methods and Table 1](https://sites.pitt.edu/~velankar/www/papers/ptLievano2017.pdf)

## Horizontal cylinder geometry

Bokányi-Tóth and coauthors extracted the experimental midplane liquid interfaces from calibrated photographs. At 0 V, 500 cSt silicone oil between equal copper cylinders had A/r² = 4.07. The cylinder radius was 1 mm. Table 4 gives half the surface gap as 0.53 mm, making the full gap 1.06 mm. The reported contact-angle parameter is 0.40 rad, surface tension 0.0212 N/m, density 970 kg/m³, and Bond number 0.45. Figure 7(a) contains experimental interface coordinates plotted alongside model predictions. An independent test would prescribe the photographed area and compare the predicted upper and lower contours with the experimental markers. The finite Bond number makes gravity material. The three other areas in the CSV belong to experiments with imposed electric fields. These are retained as out-of-scope records. No mechanical force was measured in this series. [Primary study, section 4.4, Tables 3–4 and Figure 7](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2026.12035)

## Liao parallel-cylinder force series

Liao et al. (2026) report increasing force with increasing liquid volume and decreasing force with increasing separation. The publisher identifies a spreadsheet for Figs. 5, 7, 8 and 9–13. Its publicly listed supplement at review was a DOCX for supplementary Figs. S1–S3. We could not verify the spreadsheet rows. The figures mix experimental data, Surface Evolver values and, in Figs. 11 and 13, Princen predictions. Force magnitude, wetted axial extent and replicate uncertainty must be verified before comparing an experimental total load with a simulated line load. The field-source or matched-water drainage-route hypothesis remains experimentally open. [Publisher data availability statement and supporting-information list](https://doi.org/10.1002/aic.70229)
