# Stage31a packing-replication bridge

## Question

Do the low-concentration, topology-dependent permeability effects that passed
CPD2-to-CPD4 solution verification remain meaningful relative to independent
sand-packing variability?

## Why this bridge is required

Stage30a closed the selected hydraulic-resolution question in one fixed,
mechanics-qualified 216-grain packing. Stage23c quantified clean hydraulic
packing variability, and Stage24c included a rigid-geometry packing holdout,
but neither qualifies mechanics-prepared low-concentration fibre networks in
independent sand packings. Therefore a population or concentration-law claim
would be premature.

## Frozen first step

- Reuse the independently verified Stage25 pure-sand preparation algorithm.
- Fix the preparation friction coefficient at 0.60, the Stage25 candidate used
  by the accepted Stage26x/Stage27/Stage30 chain.
- Prepare only the two unused N=216 seeds: 202608232 and 202608233.
- Retain the Stage25 stress, equilibrium, contact-network, overlap, void-ratio,
  PSD, particle-count, and finite-state gates without relaxation.
- Preserve the accepted seed 202608231 state as the primary replicate; do not
  rerun it.

## Later steps, not authorized by this checkpoint

After both new pure-sand states pass, a separate geometry stage may construct
matched one-, two-, and four-fibre mechanics-qualified networks in all three
packings. A paired CPD2 hydraulic screen may follow only after independent
geometry and mechanics audit. CPD4 is reserved for a bounded representative
subset because Stage30a already established small CPD2-to-CPD4 shifts.

## Claim boundary

Stage31a is pure-sand DEM replication only. It does not authorize fibre
placement, flow, permeability inference, a monotonic concentration law,
mobile sand, migration, clogging, deformation, or two-way coupling.
