# Stage36U phase-separated source bookkeeping prototype

No streaming, collision, pressure solve or particle motion. Inputs are Stage36T cell-integrated total source B and joint solid source Bs, both expressed per total cell volume, and solid fraction chi. Form integrated impulses Is=dt*Vcell*Bs and If=dt*Vcell*(B-Bs). Maintain separate momentum registers. Do not infer Is from chi*(If+Is). Reject nonzero impulse in a phase of exactly zero volume; do not divide by tiny fractions or clip them. This operation conserves source impulse and its phase allocation, but it does not supply the missing two-phase fluid update.

Tests: impulse conservation, zero solid impulse for exterior-only source, arbitrary nonzero solid source, invalid fraction, absent-phase rejection, force-time units, and scalar-register information-loss counterexample. Tolerance1e-12 for synthetic tests. Apply to all nine Stage36T packets, require roundoff conservation and exact zero solid impulse. These are algebraic tests and construction properties, not evidence of accurate hydrodynamic force. No automatic integration into existing single-population LBM: a compatible transport/pressure/interface operator would be a substantive solver change.
