# Stage26o layered-pluviation development attempt

## Physical question and bounded claim

Can two exact mobile F37 fibres acquire simultaneous persistent sand-contact
cages when placed between deterministic lower and upper sand-deposition bands?
A pass supports only this small wall-bounded preparation mechanism. It is not
bulk validation and does not support permeability, deformation, migration, or
concentration-effect claims.

## System and loading path

- Three-dimensional, dry, six-wall assembly with the accepted 216 LBS radii.
- Two rigid F37 clumps, each comprising 37 overlapping 50 micrometre spheres.
- Lower sand band: normalized initial z from 0 to 0.45; upper band: 0.57 to 1.
- Fibre pivots lie in the intervening fall zone and remain mobile throughout.
- Gravity settling, six-wall confinement to 100 Pa, gravity removal, then an
  active-servo zero-gravity hold use the same controls as mixture attempt 01.

## Observables and conventions

- Direct right/top/front reaction stress divided by current wall area, Pa,
  compression positive.
- Unbalanced force, kinetic energy normalized by pV, sand coordination and
  rattler fraction, maximum overlap divided by the smaller sphere radius.
- Distinct sand contacts per fibre, fibre-wall contacts, translation in m and
  D50, rotation in degrees, parent positions, member bounds, and leakage.

## Fixed physics and omissions

The Cundall-Strack frictional contact law, material parameters, radii, member
geometry, timestep controls, damping, wall friction, and stress servo are
unchanged. Fibres are rigid; bending, adhesion, electrostatics, water, air
drag, pore pressure, flow, and chemical degradation remain omitted. Numerical
damping is a preparation control, not a calibrated polymer property.

## Acceptance and failure classification

The final state requires finite values, direct mean stress within 5%, each
principal wall stress within 10%, unbalanced force <=0.03, KE/(pV) <=1e-5,
sand coordination >=3, rattlers <=0.35, overlap <=0.02, exactly 216 sand
particles, exact 2x37 fibre members of 50 micrometre radius, no sand or fibre
leakage, no fibre-wall support, and at least three distinct sand contacts per
fibre for five consecutive blocks. Penetration above 0.05 is a hard stop.

Attempt 01 remains development evidence. This layered attempt is not an
independent validation case and cannot authorize flow unless it passes and is
independently audited.
